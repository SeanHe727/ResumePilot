1. **Remove or correct the “new merchant” bullet under the hospital consulting practicum.**
   - The final bullet under **MBA Consulting Practicum** is nearly identical to the Harbor Payments onboarding bullet.
   - It refers to merchants, even though the project is for a regional hospital network.

   - Keep your phone, email, LinkedIn, and portfolio only if those links are real and current.

3. **Fix the late-delivery metric.**
   - The bullet says late deliveries fell from 11% to 7% by excluding weather-delayed shipments from the calculation.
   - That does not clearly demonstrate an operational improvement; it changes the measurement method.
   - A hiring manager may interpret this as manipulating the metric rather than improving performance.
   - Either replace the bullet with a genuinely improved outcome or clearly explain the analytical/reporting purpose of the change. Do not present a denominator or definition change as a performance gain.

4. **Clarify the scope and causality of the Harbor Payments metrics.**
   - “Improved checkout conversion by 35%” is very strong, but the bullet does not specify the time period, sample size, test design, or whether the result was measured through an experiment.

   - The S&OP bullet uses present tense—“prepares”—while the role is in the past.
   - “Analysed” is British spelling while the resume otherwise uses American context and terminology. Use one spelling convention consistently.
   - Check all bullets for consistent past tense, especially because the resume includes both completed and ongoing projects.
