# Resume review

## Overall diagnosis

Your strongest story is **operations/product work with measurable process improvement, experimentation, and cross-functional leadership**. The resume currently weakens that story in four ways:

1. **There is no summary or target-role signal.** A recruiter may see “Diesel Mechanic” first and assume you are pursuing marine maintenance rather than product or operations.
2. **The experience order is incorrect.** The 2024 product internship appears after a 2025-present role and a 2018–2023 role.
3. **The Harbor Payments section contains duplication.** Several bullets describe the same checkout/onboarding work and make the internship look padded.
4. **A few claims need correction or clarification.** The food-app growth percentage is mathematically inconsistent, and some product metrics lack enough context to establish what you personally did.

I’m assuming you are targeting **product management, associate product management, product operations, or operations strategy roles**. If you are targeting another role, the prioritization would change.

# Highest-priority changes

## 1. Add a concise summary

Add a summary near the top that immediately establishes:

- Your target function
- Your MBA status
- Your prior operations experience
- Your product internship and product-building experience
- Your strongest quantified evidence

### Why

Without a summary, the reader has to infer your intended direction from a non-obvious sequence: economics, operations analyst, MBA, product internship, diesel mechanic, and student venture. The current job title is likely to dominate the first impression.

Do not use the summary to repeat every achievement. Its main job is to explain the narrative: **operations and product candidate with experience improving workflows, launching products, using experimentation, and coordinating cross-functional teams.**

## 2. Correct the experience order

Use reverse chronological order:

1. Coastal Marine Services — Jul 2025–Present
2. Harbor Payments — Jun 2024–Aug 2024
3. Crestline Logistics — Jul 2018–Aug 2023

You can also consider separating the internship into a more prominent “Product Experience” subsection if product roles are your target, but do not hide the chronology.

### Why

The current ordering makes the document look mechanically inconsistent. Recruiters often interpret incorrect chronology as carelessness, especially for operations and product roles where prioritization and organization matter.

## 3. Fix the Campus Food Rescue growth claim

The increase from 400 to 1,150 weekly active users is not a 150% increase. It is:

- An increase of 750 users
- A 187.5% increase relative to 400
- 287.5% of the original level

### Why

This is the most obvious numerical credibility problem in the resume. A hiring manager may question the accuracy of your other metrics after noticing it.

## 4. Remove or consolidate duplicate Harbor Payments bullets

The following bullets overlap substantially:

- The checkout conversion improvement
- The A/B test methodology
- The statement about reporting the five-point lift
- The onboarding results and follow-up roadmap

### Why

The section currently spends too much space on one experiment and repeats the same result. This makes the internship appear less substantial than it probably was. Keep distinct evidence of:

- Product discovery
- Experimentation
- Roadmap prioritization
- Stakeholder communication
- Business impact

Each should appear once, with a clearly different purpose.

## 5. Make the target function visible in the skills and section structure

Your skills section currently has useful material, but it is too small and generic for product applications. In particular:

- “Product” is a useful group name.
- “Tools” is generic and does not show what each tool supported.
- “Requirements writing” should be supported by a corresponding experience bullet.
- “Amplitude” should be supported by evidence of product analytics or funnel analysis.
- “Jira” should be supported by evidence of backlog, sprint, or delivery work if you want it to carry weight.

### Why

A recruiter will not award much credit for tools listed without evidence elsewhere in the resume. Skills should reinforce the story rather than introduce unsupported capabilities.
