4. **Improve targeting.** For quant research roles, put the most relevant technical skills and research interests more prominently. For academic roles, emphasize publications, presentations, teaching, and mathematical/statistical contributions.
5. **Add missing context.** You should include dissertation topic/advisor, publications or working papers, and possibly relevant coursework if applying before graduation.
