# Resume Review

## Overall assessment

This is a strong early-career product resume with unusually good quantified outcomes. The strongest evidence is in the Harbor Payments role: you show experimentation, customer research, requirements work, cross-functional execution, and measurable business results.

The main problems are:

1. **Your target role is implied rather than stated.** The resume points toward Product Manager or Associate Product Manager roles, but the summary/header is missing.
2. **The strongest product evidence is buried below the first glance.**
3. **The skills section is too thin for product roles.**
4. **Some bullets describe activity before clarifying product judgment, ownership, or business impact.**
5. **A few claims need more precise scope or context to avoid sounding inflated or ambiguous.**
6. **Your five-year operations background is valuable, but the resume does not explicitly connect it to product management.**

You have enough relevant experience for a strong one-page early-career product resume, but the metrics are valuable. Content quality should take priority over an overly compressed layout.
