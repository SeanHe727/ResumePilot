## Overall assessment

This is a strong, metrics-driven resume with clear product-management relevance. The main improvements are to:

- Reduce the Harbor internship to 4–5 bullets so the highest-impact work stands out.
- Clarify your personal contribution where outcomes could be interpreted as team results.
- Avoid causal claims when the evidence only shows correlation.
- Standardize US spelling, currency, capitalization, dates, and number formatting.
- Update anything labeled “Present” if it is no longer current.
- Add missing technical or analytical skills only if you genuinely used them and can discuss them in an interview.
- Keep the final document to one page for MBA product roles.

## Formatting and consistency checks

- Use one date format throughout and choose either an en dash or hyphen consistently.
- Use one convention for numbers; currently some numbers are spelled out while others use numerals.
- Standardize US English if US employers are the target: spelling, units, and currency.
- Ensure bullets do not break awkwardly across lines in the final PDF.
- Use consistent serial commas, especially in lists of functions or stakeholders.
- Keep punctuation consistent across all bullets.
- Check that every URL is clickable and that the PDF filename is professional.
- Verify that the resume remains legible at normal zoom and fits on one page.
