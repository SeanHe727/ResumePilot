## Highest-priority fixes

1. **Remove the duplicate dispute-feature bullet**
   These two Harbor Payments bullets say essentially the same thing:
   - “Wrote the requirements and success metrics…”
   - “Drafted the specification and target metrics…”

   Keep the stronger first version.

2. **Delete or correct the “team of 40/P&L” claim**
   > “Managed a team of 40 people… and owned the company’s P&L…”

   This is difficult to believe for a three-month APM internship and conflicts with the scope shown elsewhere. Remove it unless it is completely accurate. If you coordinated rather than directly managed the group, state that precisely:
   > Coordinated a 40-person cross-functional working group across product and engineering…

   Only mention P&L ownership if you genuinely had decision-making accountability for it.

3. **Correct the impossible growth metric**
   > “Grew weekly active users from 1,000 to 900, a 90% increase…”

   Going from 1,000 to 900 is a **10% decrease**. Verify the underlying numbers. For example:
   - 500 to 950 = 90% increase
   - 900 to 1,000 = 11% increase
   - 1,000 to 1,900 = 90% increase

4. **Fix the practicum dates**
   > May 2024 – Jan 2024

   The end date precedes the start date. Correct the year or reverse the dates.

5. **Remove the unrelated duplicate onboarding bullet**
   The merchant-onboarding bullet under the hospital practicum is copied from Harbor Payments and does not fit a hospital project. Replace it with an actual practicum accomplishment.

6. **Correct tense and spelling**
   - “Coordinates” → **Coordinated**
   - “roadmaping” → **roadmapping**
   - “user interveiws” → **user interviews**

## Education and formatting

- If the MBA was not yet completed when applying, write:
  > MBA Candidate, expected June 2025
- If completed, simply use:
  > Master of Business Administration, June 2025
- Use consistent date punctuation, preferably:
  > Sep 2023 – Jun 2025
- Keep bullets to one or two lines where possible.
- Use consistent geographic and numerical style: either “2 sites” throughout or “two sites” throughout. Numerals are usually easier to scan on a resume.
- Make sure the LinkedIn URL is clickable and customized.
- Use consistent commas in lists: “engineering, risk, and support.”
- Aim for **3–5 bullets per role**, ordered by relevance and impact.
