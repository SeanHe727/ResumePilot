Your resume has strong quantitative results, especially in payments and operations. The biggest issues are **credibility, duplication, grammar, and unclear scope**. I would make the following changes.

## Highest-priority fixes

### 1. Correct factual inconsistencies

- **Campus Food Rescue App:**  
  “Grew weekly active users from 1,000 to 900, a 90% increase” is mathematically incorrect.  
  - If the numbers are correct, this is a **10% decrease**.
  - If the increase was 90%, the ending number should be approximately 1,900.
- **MBA Consulting Practicum:**  
  “May 2024 - Jan 2024” is impossible. It may be **May 2024 - Jan 2025**.
- **Duplicate Harbor Payments content:**  
  The dispute self-service accomplishment appears twice:
  - “Wrote the requirements and success metrics…”
  - “Drafted the specification and target metrics…”
  
  Keep only one.
- **Incorrect project bullet:**  
  The merchant onboarding accomplishment under the hospital consulting practicum clearly belongs under **Harbor Payments**. Remove it from the project section.
- **MBA dates:**  
  If you have already graduated, change “Sep 2023 - Jun 2025” to the appropriate completed date. If you are still enrolled, use “Expected Jun 2025.”

### 2. Reconsider the “team of 40” and P&L bullet

This bullet is likely to raise questions:

> Managed a team of 40 people across product and engineering and owned the company’s P&L for the payments business.

It is unusually broad for an **Associate Product Manager Intern** and conflicts with the rest of the role. If accurate, clarify the scope:

> Coordinated work across a 40-person product and engineering organization and contributed to payments-business planning.

If you truly owned a P&L, specify the size and what “owned” meant. Otherwise, remove it. Unsupported or overstated claims can hurt credibility.

### 3. Rewrite the long logistics bullet

This sentence contains too many responsibilities and no clear outcome:

> Led the rollout of a route-planning tool to 3 depots while also organizing training sessions, rewriting the standard operating procedures...

Break it into one or two bullets and add metrics:

> Led rollout of a route-planning tool across 3 depots; trained operations teams and rewrote SOPs to support adoption.  
> - Negotiated with the vendor and tracked implementation metrics, reducing driver hours by **X%** and improving route adherence by **Y%**.

If you do not have the metrics, prioritize the implementation scope and remove subjective language such as “improved morale” and “made daily operations smoother.”

### 4. Use consistent tense

Your current role/project bullets mix past and present tense:

- “Rebuilt,” “Cut,” “Led” — past tense
- “Coordinates,” “prepares” — present tense

Use:
- **Present tense** for current roles
- **Past tense** for completed roles

For example:

> Coordinate the quarterly S&OP review across sales, finance, and operations; prepare the forecast pack for each meeting.

Or, if the role is completed:

> Coordinated the quarterly S&OP review across sales, finance, and operations and prepared the forecast pack for each meeting.
