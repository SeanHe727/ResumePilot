### 1. Remove weak, vague, or non-credible bullets

Delete or rewrite these:

- “Leveraged cutting-edge AI synergies to drive innovation across the organization.”
- “Single-handedly built the company’s entire ML platform used by every team.”
- “Responsible for the monitoring dashboards of the diagnostics service.”
- “I worked on improving the latency of our API and I also wrote tests for it.”
- The duplicate fine-tuning bullet in the Mobility Systems role.

These either sound generic, use first person, lack measurable impact, or make claims that recruiters may question.

Your experience is already in reverse chronological order, but the strongest role should lead with its highest-impact bullet:

1. Mobility Systems Company — ML Engineering Intern  
2. Eastern Robotics Co. — Software Engineer Intern  

For technical roles, place **Experience before Education** unless you are targeting research positions or have very limited work experience.

### 3. Add missing links

Your contact line currently includes a code URL, but it is not clear whether this is GitHub. Use explicit labels:


### 4. Make every technology claim specific

Several technologies appear only in the Skills section. Move the technologies used in important accomplishments into the bullets themselves.

