## Improve readability

Several bullets are technically impressive but too dense. Aim for approximately two lines per bullet and one primary accomplishment per bullet.

Could become:

> Built an ML diagnostics workflow that triaged 800+ sensor signals per case using structured feature extraction, evidence-grounded review, and abstention for low-confidence findings.  
> Contributed to a 68% reduction in the inspection system’s pending-case backlog after launch.

## Formatting and consistency

Use these conventions throughout:

- Past tense for completed roles and projects; present tense only for ongoing work.
- Either abbreviate all months or spell all months out.
- Use en dashes consistently: `Oct 2024 – May 2025`.
- Avoid first-person language such as “I worked on.”
- Prevent awkward line-break hyphenation such as `held-` on one line and `out` on the next.
- Define metrics precisely: “F1 score,” “p95 latency,” or “citation accuracy,” not “performance.”
- Keep the resume to one page if possible for internship or early-career applications.
