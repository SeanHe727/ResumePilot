> Valued the acquisition target by discounting its free cash flow to equity at the company’s weighted average cost of capital.

**Problem**
1. [Error] The line mismatches the cash-flow measure and discount rate: FCFE is discounted at the cost of equity, not WACC.
2. [Important] The valuation method is given without a valuation output or other stated result.

**Why**
1. Free cash flow to equity is cash available to equity holders, so its discount rate is the cost of equity. WACC is used to discount free cash flow to the firm; the mismatch makes the valuation method technically incorrect as written.
2. A reader cannot gauge the result or its significance from the method alone. Without an estimate or the decision it informed, the value of the work remains difficult to assess.

**How to change it**
1. If the model used FCFE, replace “weighted average cost of capital” with “cost of equity.” If it used WACC, replace “free cash flow to equity” with “free cash flow to the firm,” if that is what was modeled.
2. Add [valuation estimate or decision informed, if shareable] after the method.

> Reported a 45% group EBITDA margin by adding the EBITDA margins of the three business units.

**Problem**
1. [Error] Adding the three business-unit EBITDA margins does not calculate a group EBITDA margin.
2. [Polish] The margin is reported without saying what it informed, and the calculation explanation follows the figure.

**Why**
1. The group margin is combined EBITDA divided by combined revenue, or equivalently a revenue-weighted average of the unit margins. Simply adding the margins is not a valid calculation of that measure.

**How to change it**
1. Calculate the group margin as combined EBITDA divided by combined revenue, and use that result; if 45% came from adding the unit margins, remove or correct it.
