Your strongest material is the quantified finance work. Before polishing it, fix **three credibility issues**: the valuation method, the EBITDA margin calculation, and the cash-forecast bullet repeated under the student fund.

### Header and education
- **Name and contact line:** Keep these. Check that the profile link works and points to a professional, up-to-date page.
- **Date of birth and nationality:** Remove the birth date; it generally does not help a U.S. resume and can invite bias. Include nationality only if it answers a relevant work-authorization requirement.

### Stonebridge Manufacturing
- **40% close-efficiency bullet:** Specify what improved by 40%—time to close, hours spent, or another measure. The result is strong, but the metric is currently unclear.
- **12-project capital-plan bullet:** Clarify your decision-making contribution and the outcome, not just the standardized template. Also check whether applying one discount rate to every project was appropriate for their differing risks.
- **Board-pack commentary bullet:** Explain the scale or value of the work—such as how many lines you covered or what decisions the commentary supported. “Each line more than 5% off budget” describes a rule, not your impact.
- **Acquisition-valuation bullet:** Correct the methodology. Free cash flow **to equity** is generally discounted at the **cost of equity**, not WACC. If you used WACC, verify whether the cash flow was actually free cash flow **to the firm**. This error will stand out to a finance reviewer.
- **45% EBITDA-margin bullet:** Recalculate or remove this claim. Adding business-unit EBITDA margins does not produce a group margin; the group margin must reflect the units’ underlying EBITDA and revenue. State your actual contribution only after verifying the figure.
- **18-month forecast bullet:** Keep this prominent. Verify that the under-4% error and USD 40M early repayment are documented and that the forecast genuinely enabled the repayment, rather than merely coinciding with it.
