1. **Remove the practicum bullet about merchant onboarding.** It repeats an achievement from Harbor Payments and does not fit the hospital-network project. As written, it looks like a copy-paste error and raises questions about accuracy.
2. **Reconsider the late-delivery bullet.** Excluding weather-delayed shipments from the on-time calculation may look like changing the metric rather than improving delivery performance. Show a genuine operational outcome, or explain the adjusted metric clearly and transparently.
3. **Fix the unclear onboarding-measurement bullet.** It describes a comparison but gives no result; because merchants chose whether to opt in, the comparison may also be affected by selection bias. Include what you learned or accomplished, or remove the bullet.

5. **Remove date of birth.** Nationality is generally unnecessary on a U.S. résumé unless an application specifically requests it. Share work-authorization information only if it is relevant and accurate.
6. **Correct the tense inconsistency** in the Crestline S&OP bullet and check that dates, units, and spelling are consistent throughout.

- **Merchant interviews and chargeback analysis:** This is one of the strongest strategy-oriented bullets. Specify the currency for “1.1 million dollars” and ensure the estimate is clearly annualized. - **Add a brief, target-specific summary or headline if it helps connect your operations background to product work.** The résumé currently makes the reader infer that transition. Keep the summary evidence-based and short; don’t use it to repeat the bullets.
- **Reorder bullets within roles.** Put the most relevant, strongest outcomes first. In particular, move the onboarding outcome up in the Harbor section and remove the duplicate from the practicum.
- **Use consistent U.S. English.** The résumé uses “analysed,” while the roles and contact details suggest U.S. applications. Standardize spelling, date style, punctuation, and number/unit formatting.
- **Check length and layout in the final document.** The supplied text does not reveal page breaks or visual hierarchy. For an early-career product résumé, prioritize keeping the strongest experience easy to scan and avoid letting projects crowd out paid work.
