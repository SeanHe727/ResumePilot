Your strongest material is the quantified product and operations work. The main fixes are to **remove misleading metrics, separate measured results from claims of causation, and delete a misplaced duplicate bullet**. I’ve kept this to changes and reasons, without rewriting your lines.

### Header and education
- **Contact line:** Make sure the LinkedIn URL and contact details are your real, working details if these are placeholders. Recruiters need to be able to reach you.
- **Date of birth and nationality:** Remove both for a typical US application. They are generally unnecessary and can invite bias.

### Harbor Payments

2. **Dispute self-service:** Keep the cross-functional work, but clarify whether you owned the requirements, the delivery, or both. The early ship date is useful only if the planned date was established beforehand.
