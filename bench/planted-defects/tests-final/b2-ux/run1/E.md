## Highest-priority changes

- **Header — remove date of birth and nationality** unless an application specifically requires them. They aren’t needed to assess your UX research experience and disclose personal information.
- **Pinegrove accessibility bullet — verify and correct the WCAG claim.** A 3:1 contrast ratio applies to large text at WCAG 2.1 AA; ordinary text generally requires 4.5:1. Also, meeting a text-contrast threshold alone doesn’t establish that an entire design system meets WCAG 2.1 AA. Make the claim match the checks you actually performed and their scope.
- **Northwind NPS bullet — check the metric name.** NPS is not the average of 0–10 recommendation ratings; it is calculated from the share of promoters minus the share of detractors. If 6.1 and 8.4 are average ratings, don’t label them NPS.
- **Transit map results — correct the percentages and reduce duplication.** Going from 90 to 60 seconds is a **33% reduction in time**, not a 50% improvement. Going from 65% to 85% task success is a **20-percentage-point increase** (about a 31% relative increase). The two bullets also credit the same design changes with separate results, so present the relationship between the findings clearly rather than making the outcomes sound like duplicate claims.
- **Skills — fix the typo** in “interveiws.” It is an easy-to-spot proofreading error.
