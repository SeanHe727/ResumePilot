## Highest-priority changes

1. **Remove your date of birth and nationality.**  
   They are generally unnecessary for UX research applications and can introduce privacy and discrimination concerns. Include them only if a specific application or jurisdiction requires them.

2. **Correct the accessibility claim.**  
   “At least 3:1” is not sufficient for normal body text under WCAG 2.1 AA. Normal-sized text generally requires **4.5:1** contrast; 3:1 applies to large text and certain other cases. This is an important technical error because the current wording suggests the design system met AA when it may not have.

3. **Correct the NPS metric.**  
   NPS is not the average 0–10 recommendation rating. It is calculated from the percentages of promoters minus detractors. Your line currently describes an average rating, not NPS. Either verify that the metric was actually NPS or relabel the metric accurately and state how it was measured.

4. **Fix tense and grammar inconsistencies.**  
   Your former roles should generally use past tense. The bullets beginning with “Synthesizing” and “Writes” are inconsistent with the rest of the resume.

5. **Correct the route-finding percentage.**  
   A decrease from 90 seconds to 60 seconds is a **33.3% reduction**, not a 50% improvement. If you retain “50%,” you need a different, clearly defined calculation. Also distinguish between percentage points and percentage change in the task-success bullet.
