# Resume review

You have strong, concrete operational results—especially the stockout, freight, dock-wait, and inventory-accuracy improvements. The main opportunities are to sharpen the role story, clarify a few results that could raise questions, and replace responsibility-focused bullets with evidence of impact.

## Changes to make first

1. **Put your experience in reverse chronological order.** Move Crestway Foods above Lakeview Distribution. Your current Greenleaf role should remain first.
2. **Clarify the Crestway lead-time/WIP result.** Doubling work-in-process while reducing lead time may seem counterintuitive to operations hiring managers. Explain what WIP measured, why it increased, and whether the lead-time improvement came with a tradeoff.
3. **Resolve the duplicate-looking food bank and Crestway metrics.** Both entries cite 1,400 products across three warehouses and nearly $2M less inventory alongside a large stockout reduction. If these are separate projects, make their distinct scopes and results clear. If they describe the same work, include the result only once and attribute it accurately.
4. **Review the OEE figure above 100%.** It may be valid depending on how performance was calculated, but readers may question the metric or the baseline. Clarify the calculation or use a measure that is unambiguous.
5. **Add outcomes to bullets that currently describe responsibilities.** Several bullets explain what you owned or built, but not what changed because of it.

## Header and section order

- **Header:** Add your city or region near your contact details, and make sure the LinkedIn address is a complete, working link. For an operations-analytics target, add a GitHub, portfolio, or project link only if it contains relevant work, such as the routing model.
- **Section order:** Given your work history, consider placing Experience before Education. Keep Projects prominent if you’re targeting analyst or industrial-engineering roles and the projects are relevant to the job.
- **Career direction:** The move from supply chain planning to florist delivery is noticeable. You don’t need to justify it, but if you’re applying back to supply chain or analytics roles, make your target clear through the roles and skills you emphasize. A short, specific summary could help explain that direction; a generic summary would add little.
- **Formatting:** Keep dates, location formatting, punctuation, and bullet indentation consistent. Avoid awkward line breaks in the final document.
