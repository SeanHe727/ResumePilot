## Highest-priority corrections

- **Median survival estimate:** The method described—averaging follow-up times for patients who died and excluding censored patients—does not correctly estimate median survival. Verify the analysis and describe the estimator actually used. If this was the method, don’t present the result as a valid median survival estimate.
- **Odd/even allocation:** Assigning treatment by admission day is predictable and is not true random assignment. Check the study protocol and describe the design accurately; don’t call it randomized unless allocation was genuinely randomized.
- **Asthma readmission calculation:** A change from 12% to 8% is a **4-percentage-point decrease**, or about a **33% relative reduction**, not 50%. Also, “cut” implies causation; use a causal claim only if the study design supports it.
- **Asthma survey-response calculation:** A rise from 35% to 55% is **20 percentage points**, not a 20% increase. It is about a 57% relative increase. Make clear what comparison supports attributing the change to SMS reminders.
