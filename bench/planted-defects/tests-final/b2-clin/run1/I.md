## Highest-priority corrections

Before submitting, fix these four credibility issues:

2. **Odd/even admission-day assignment:** This is predictable allocation, not true randomization. Describe the study design accurately and do not list this as randomization. Predictable assignment creates selection bias and possible calendar-related confounding.

4. **Survey-response arithmetic:** A rise from 35% to 55% is a **20-percentage-point increase**, not simply a 20% increase. The relative increase is about 57%.
