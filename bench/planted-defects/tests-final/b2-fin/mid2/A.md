2. [Polish] The acquisition-valuation bullet does not state its output or what it informed.

> Reported a 45% group EBITDA margin by adding the EBITDA margins of the three business units.

**Problem**
1. [Error] Adding the three unit EBITDA margins does not calculate a group EBITDA margin.
2. [Polish] The reported group margin has no stated effect or use.

**Why**
1. Adding unit margin percentages does not produce a group margin. The group margin is total group EBITDA divided by total group revenue, equivalent to a revenue-weighted average of the unit margins.

**How to change it**
1. Calculate group EBITDA margin as total EBITDA divided by total revenue; use 45% only if that calculation produces 45%.
