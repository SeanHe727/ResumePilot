### Stonebridge Manufacturing | Financial Analyst

- **“Improved close efficiency by 40%…”** Define what “close efficiency” measures—such as elapsed close time or hours spent—and the before-and-after baseline. Clarify your specific contribution to the automation and whether the 40% is attributable to it.
- **Capital plan for 12 plant projects:** Say what you did with the analysis and, if available, what decisions or outcomes it informed. “So every request used…” describes the template’s intended benefit, but doesn’t establish that teams adopted it.
- **Monthly board-pack commentary:** Specify whether “each line more than 5% off budget” means every variance above a 5% threshold or all lines in the pack. Consider clarifying the basis for the threshold and your role in preparing the commentary.
- **Acquisition valuation:** This contains a technical error: **free cash flow to equity is normally discounted at the cost of equity, not WACC**. Verify whether you actually used FCFE or instead used a different cash-flow measure. Correct the underlying methodology and terminology before keeping the claim.
- **“Reported a 45% group EBITDA margin by adding…”** This is likely mathematically invalid. Business-unit EBITDA margins generally cannot be added to produce a consolidated margin; the group margin should be calculated from aggregated EBITDA and revenue. Recheck the calculation and the reported figure. If you only summed the unit margins, remove or correct this claim.
- **18-month cash-flow forecast:** Define how forecast error was calculated and over what period. Clarify whether the USD 40M debt repayment was enabled by your forecast or merely happened afterward. This appears again in the Student Investment Fund section; verify whether the duplication is intentional and whether both roles can legitimately claim the same achievement.

### Crescent Bank | Credit Analyst
