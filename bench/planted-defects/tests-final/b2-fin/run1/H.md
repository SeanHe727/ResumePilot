## Highest-priority changes

1. **Correct the technical valuation error.**  
   The acquisition bullet says you discounted free cash flow to equity at the company’s WACC. FCFE should generally be discounted at the cost of equity; WACC is normally used with unlevered free cash flow. Verify which cash-flow measure and discount rate you actually used and make them consistent.

2. **Remove or correct the EBITDA-margin bullet.**  
   Adding the EBITDA margins of three business units does not produce a valid group EBITDA margin. A consolidated margin must be calculated using total EBITDA divided by total revenue, with appropriate weighting. This bullet could seriously undermine your finance credibility if left unchanged.

3. **Resolve the duplicated cash-flow achievement.**  
   The USD 600M forecast, 4% error, and USD 40M debt repayment appear in both Stonebridge Manufacturing and Student Investment Fund. The dates also make the duplication questionable: the fund project predates the manufacturing role. Determine which experience actually produced the result and keep it only there.

4. **Fix the tense inconsistency.**  
   “Writes the covenant-monitoring checklist” is present tense even though Crescent Bank ended in December 2022. Use past tense for completed work. Also check that all bullets consistently use past tense for previous roles and present tense only for ongoing work.

5. **Make the current caddie role intentional.**  
   It may look like an unexplained step backward from financial analyst to caddie, especially because it is listed first and is your current role. If this reflects a career break, temporary work, relocation, or a deliberate transition, address that elsewhere in the application or make the role’s relevance clearer. Otherwise, recruiters may focus on the unexplained chronology rather than your finance experience.
