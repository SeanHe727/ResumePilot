> Valued the acquisition target by discounting its free cash flow to equity at the company’s weighted average cost of capital.

**Problem**
[Error] The valuation line uses an incorrect FCFE discount rate and gives no valuation result or decision impact.

**Why**
Free cash flow to equity is cash available to equity holders after financing flows and should generally be discounted at the cost of equity. WACC is appropriate for free cash flow to the firm, so the current pairing mixes incompatible cash-flow and discount-rate definitions; without a value or decision, the method overwhelms the accomplishment.

**How to change it**
Use free cash flow to equity discounted at the cost of equity, or use free cash flow to the firm discounted at WACC. Then add [implied valuation or valuation range] compared with [asking price, offer, or other decision benchmark] and [acquisition decision, recommendation, or negotiation outcome] if accurate.

> Reported a 45% group EBITDA margin by adding the EBITDA margins of the three business units.

**Problem**
[Error] The reported group EBITDA margin is wrong because it adds business-unit margins instead of calculating a revenue-weighted group margin.

**Why**
Margins are ratios and cannot be added across business units to produce a group margin. The correct group margin is total EBITDA divided by total revenue, equivalent to a revenue-weighted average of the unit margins; the current line therefore gives no reliable denominator or weighting and no resulting management impact.

**How to change it**
Recalculate the figure as total group EBITDA divided by total group revenue, or as the revenue-weighted average of the three unit margins, and replace 45% with [resulting percentage]. Add [planning, performance-review, or management decision enabled] if accurate.
