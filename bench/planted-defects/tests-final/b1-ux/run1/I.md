## Overall structure and presentation

- **Consider moving Education below Experience and Projects.** Your professional experience is now more important than your degree.
- **Keep the resume to one or two pages based on actual layout.** Do not compress it so tightly that the portfolio and metrics become hard to scan.
- **Avoid manual line breaks inside bullets.** Let the document wrap naturally to prevent formatting problems in applicant-tracking systems.
- **Use consistent date formatting, punctuation, and location formatting.**
- **Make every metric defensible.** Be ready to explain baseline, sample, time period, data source, and whether the result was relative or absolute.
- **Emphasize research contribution and decision impact.** Several bullets currently emphasize interface changes, making your profile read partly like a product designer’s resume.
- **Trim weaker activity-based content before cutting quantified outcomes.** The first transit bullet, the survey/dashboard bullet, and the repository bullet need the most strengthening.
- **Do not add a summary unless you need to establish a specific specialization.** Your progression and accessibility focus are already visible without one.
