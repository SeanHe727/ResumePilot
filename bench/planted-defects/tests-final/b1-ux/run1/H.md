1. **Correct the WCAG claim.** A 3:1 contrast ratio is generally sufficient for large text, not normal body text under WCAG 2.1 AA. This is the most serious factual issue because it could undermine your accessibility credibility.
2. **Remove or resolve the duplicated onboarding bullet.** The clinic project contains a nearly identical result to the Pinegrove bullet, but the context and metric do not match.
3. **Replace vague language with evidence.** Several bullets use phrases such as “meaningful, user-first outcomes,” “owned,” and “championed” without showing what changed.
4. **Break up the overloaded Northwind bullet.** It combines research, recruiting, documentation, internship management, accessibility work, and an outcome. It is difficult to understand your contribution.
5. **Make more bullets show the research-to-impact connection.** Your strongest bullets do this well; several others stop at describing an activity.
