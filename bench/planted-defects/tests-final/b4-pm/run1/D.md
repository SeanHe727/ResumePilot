## Overall assessment

The main weakness is the **story the resume tells at a glance**: your most recent job is Diesel Mechanic, while most of your product experience is an internship and student projects. ### Harbor Payments — Associate Product Manager Intern

This is your most directly relevant experience and should be easy to find. The six bullets are a lot for a three-month internship, and some overlap; prioritize the distinct outcomes and methods.

- **Onboarding bullet:** Strong evidence of product impact: it includes a change, a time-based result, and the pilot population. Clarify whether the time reduction was measured across all 2,300 merchants or in another subset, if that distinction matters.

Clarify the A/B test’s scope and whether “shipping it to all merchants” means the tested flow was rolled out after the experiment.

- **A/B test methodology bullet:** It overlaps heavily with the checkout-conversion bullet. Keep the methodology only if it demonstrates something important not apparent from the result bullet, such as experimental rigor or how you interpreted the effect. Also distinguish the 5-percentage-point result from a relative percentage change.
